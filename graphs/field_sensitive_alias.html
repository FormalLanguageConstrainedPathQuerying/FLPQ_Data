Canonical grammars#

Productions with index \(i\) are duplicated for each field number from the analyzed program classes. The start nonterminal is \(V\). Reversed edges (a_r, d_r, f_r_i) are auto-generated from forward edges.

\[\begin{split}M \, \rightarrow \, d_r \, V \, d \, \\ V \, \rightarrow \, A_r \, V \mid V \, A \mid f_{r\_i} \, V \, f_i \mid M \mid \varepsilon \, \\ A_r \, \rightarrow \, M \, a_r \mid a_r \mid \varepsilon \, \\ A \, \rightarrow \, a \, M \mid a \mid \varepsilon \, \\\end{split}\]
