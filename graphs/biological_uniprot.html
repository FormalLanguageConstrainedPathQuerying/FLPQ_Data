Biological graphs from UniProt#

Release:

6.1.2

Date:

Oct 28, 2026

Biological interaction graphs derived from the UniProt knowledgebase, where nodes are biological entities and edges are the relations between them (e.g. belongs_to, is_homologous_to); they support subgraph and path queries as in “Subgraph queries by context-free grammars”.
