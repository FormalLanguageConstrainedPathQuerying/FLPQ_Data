Social Networks
***************

.. only:: html

   :Release: |release|
   :Date: |today|

Large directed graphs of social and family relations; nodes are entities
(people, places, works) and edges are social/family/affiliation
relationships (friendship, marriage, parenthood, citizenship, location).
Queries are regular path queries over those relations.
