RPQ#

Release:

6.1.2

Date:

Oct 28, 2026

Regular path queries (RPQ) constrain the paths of a labeled graph by a regular language. A query is specified by a regular expression template instantiated per graph from its stored labels; an RPQ may also be represented by a regular RSM. The graph catalog is shared across all query classes — the “Applicable graphs” list below cross-links the per-graph pages without duplicating them.
